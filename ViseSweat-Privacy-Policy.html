1. PRIVACY BY DESIGN & GLOBAL COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK

The application is structured upon strict Privacy by Design principles, operating in adherence to international data protection standards including GDPR, CCPA, and LGPD.

  • Storage & Cryptography: The system operates under a strict "Zero-Knowledge" infrastructure. All user-inputted architecture (sobriety metrics, behavioral logs, personal records) is stored exclusively within the internal local hardware partition of the user's personal device.
  • Data Monetization: The developer does not harvest, mirror, compile, or transmit telemetry metadata regarding your physical or psychological condition.

Unlike standard cloud-dependent services, all interactions occur completely outside the perimeter, control, and access of the development engineering team.

  • Connectivity Boundaries: Local logging features function fully independently of cellular networks or internet connections, mitigating remote Man-in-the-Middle (MITM) intercept risks.
  • Network Outbounds: External network traffic is limited strictly to managing transactions handled via Google Play Billing API and integrating local device native software layer requests.

3. LOCAL ACCESS RESTRICTIONS & CREDENTIAL RECOVERY

The localized security locking mechanism serves as an absolute defensive barrier against unauthorized access.

  • Absence of Master Access: The software does not maintain a centralized database, "super passwords", administrative backdoors, or credential recovery servers.
